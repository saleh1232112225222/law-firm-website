FAQs About Company Formation in Riyadh

What is the minimum capital for an LLC in Saudi Arabia?

There is no legal minimum capital requirement for LLCs, but it should be appropriate for the business activity.

Can a single person form a company in Saudi Arabia?

Yes, a single-person LLC (one-person company) is permitted under Saudi Companies Law.

How long does company formation take?

Typically 5-15 business days depending on company type and document completeness.
